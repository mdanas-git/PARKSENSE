Privacy & Data Protection

ParkSense values your privacy and ensures minimal, secure data collection for smart parking.

1. What We Store
  • Account Data: Name, verified email address, encrypted password hash, and user role.
  • Vehicle Data: Optional vehicle description and plate registration number for parking authorization.
  • Saved Locations: Your bookmarked parking facilities for quick navigation.
  • Reservation Logs: Timestamps and slot identifiers for active and historical reservations.
2. Public Anonymity Guarantee

When you reserve or park in a slot, other parkers and public viewers only see the slot status (e.g., RESERVED or OCCUPIED). Your personal identity, contact number, and vehicle registration are never displayed publicly.

3. Real Hardware Rules

Physical occupancy measured by ESP32 ultrasonic sensors always overrides reservations. If a car parks in a reserved spot, the reservation is automatically closed.

4. Data Rights & Deletion

You can export or delete your profile and personal data at any time via the Profile & Data tab.
